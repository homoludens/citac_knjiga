# Apache PDFBox and Bouncy Castle

`com.tom-roush:pdfbox-android:2.0.27.0` is an Android port of Apache PDFBox
distributed under the Apache License 2.0:
<https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0>.

Its pinned cryptographic runtime dependencies are Bouncy Castle
`bcprov-jdk15to18`, `bcpkix-jdk15to18`, and `bcutil-jdk15to18`, version `1.72`,
under the Bouncy Castle MIT-like license:
<https://www.bouncycastle.org/licence.html>.

Artifact hashes and upstream source references are recorded in
`document-pdf/pdfbox-source-closure.json` and
`pdf-qualification/android-consumer/qualification-closure.json`.
